title: Noetherian demons
---

Me and my buddy Rishi one day turned up to an quiz competition organized by the Astronomy club of IISER Mohali. There was prize money, so we both were ready. However, teams of two or three were allowed, and every other team had three members. Then we saw another of our friends, Sparsha, came up to the quiz as an audience. We pulled him onto our team and thus formed the ***Noetherian demons***, the greatest threat the Astronomy and Physics clubs in Mohali had ever faced.
