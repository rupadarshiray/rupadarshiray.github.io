> [!problem]
> Show that the cone over $X$ $$ X \hookrightarrow \mathrm{Cone}(X) $$ has the *homotopy extension property.*

> [!quote] 
> Not all inclusions  $A \subset X$ are created equal.
> ^[[math.jhu.edu/~jmb/note/hep.pdf](https://math.jhu.edu/~jmb/note/hep.pdf)]

> [!definition] Homotopy extension property 
> Let $X$ be a topological space and let $A \subset X$. We say $(X,A)$ has the **homotopy extension property** if for a homotopy $f_{\bullet}:A\times I\to Y$ and a map $g_{0}:X\to Y$ such that $f_{0}=g_{0}|_{A}$ there exists a homotopy $$ g_{\bullet}: X\times I\to Y $$ that starts from $g_{0}:X\to Y$ and *extends* $f$, that is, $$ f_{t}= g_{t} |_{A} , \forall t\in I$$
