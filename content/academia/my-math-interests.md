My primary interests lie in the topics related to the analysis of manifolds and functions on it: be it through geometric, topological, dynamical or analytical perspectives/techniques.

- I have invested a lot of time on [[inculcation-riemann-surfaces|Riemann surfaces]], hyperbolic manifolds and discrete subgroups of $\text{Isom}(\mathbb{R} \pmb{H}^{n})\cong O^{+}(1,n)$ during [[my-ms-thesis|my MS thesis]].
- Rigidity of discrete groups: Mostow-Prasad's rigidity theorem, Tukia's proof of  quasiconformal rigidity, Sullivan's proof of measurable rigidity using Patterson-Sullivan measures
- Deformations of discrete groups: quasiconformal deformations, space of discrete faithful representations $\mathrm{RepDF}(\Gamma, O^{+}(1,n))$ of finitely generated groups $\Gamma$
- Constructing such discrete subgroups for higher $n$ which various (geometric/topological/algebraic) properties

My broad interests also include geometric topology and topology/geometry of 2 and 3-manifolds, Riemannian geometry, symplectic geometry and Hamiltonian flows, complex analysis, abstract harmonic analysis, geometric and analytic theory of finitely generated and locally compact groups, representation theory of Lie groups and Lie algebras, etc.
