title: (My) math interests
---
My primary interests lie in the topics related to the analysis of manifolds and functions on it: be it through geometric, topological, dynamical or analytical perspectives/techniques.

- I have invested a lot of time on Riemann surfaces, hyperbolic manifolds and discrete subgroups of $\text{Isom}(\mathbb{R} \pmb{H}^{n})\cong O^{+}(1,n)$ during [[my-ms-thesis|my MS thesis]].
	- Rigidity of discrete groups: Mostow-Prasad's rigidity theorem, Tukia's proof of  quasiconformal rigidity, Sullivan's proof of measurable rigidity using Patterson-Sullivan measures
	- Deformations of discrete groups: quasiconformal deformations, space of discrete faithful representations $\mathrm{RepDF}(\Gamma, O^{+}(1,n))$ of finitely generated groups $\Gamma$
	- Constructing such discrete subgroups for higher $n$ which various (geometric/topological/algebraic) properties
- Complex analysis, Riemann surfaces and complex geometry, holomorphic mappings, quasiconformal mappings on spheres
- Locally compact groups, geometric and analytic group theory/abstract harmonic analysis, amenable groups, ergodic theory of actions of amenable groups, representation theory of Lie groups and Lie algebras
- Geometric and analytic theory of finitely generated groups, Gromov hyperbolic groups, boundaries of Gromov hyperbolic groups, Cannon-Thurston maps
- Geometric topology of 2 and 3-manifolds
- Riemannian geometry
- Symplectic geometry and Hamiltonian flows

I wish to someday study more about several complex variables, complex manifolds and algebraic tools used in complex geometry.
