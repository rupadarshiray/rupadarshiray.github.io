> [!abstract]
>  We look at open problems on discrete groups of $\mor{Isom}(\R\pmb{H}^{d})$ in higher dimensions $d>3$. Restricting the focus to finitely generated, free discrete subgroups of $\mor{Isom}(\R\pmb{H}^{d})$ of purely hyperbolic-type, we first look at classical (and non-classical) Schottky groups, the results of Maskit (on classification of such groups with nonempty domain of discontinuity as precisely the Schottky groups) and Chuckrow (on existence of such groups with empty domain of discontinuity) for $d=3$. Next, we look at the results of Mj on existence of Cannon-Thurston maps for finitely generated Kleinian groups. We wish to prove similar results, or more likely find counterexamples of such, for $d>3$. We look at constructions of counterexamples to finiteness theorems by Ahlfors, Sullivan; construction of fake Schottky groups etc. I studied the fundamentals of Gromov hyperbolic groups, its boundary and Cannon-Thurston extensions of group homomorphisms. The group constructed by Baker and Riley provides a counterexample in the existence of Cannon-Thurston maps in this general setting. Mj asks if we can embed this group as a cocompact (or convex cocompact) subgroup of $\mor{Isom}(\R\pmb{H}^{d})$ for $d>3$.
